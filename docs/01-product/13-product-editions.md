# MaintainPro Product Editions

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Draft
**Document Type:** Product Specification

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the commercial editions of MaintainPro.

Product Editions determine which capabilities are available to different customer segments while preserving a consistent user experience across the platform.

Editions are designed to support organizations as they grow rather than forcing unnecessary upgrades.

---

# 2. Edition Philosophy

MaintainPro follows four guiding principles for editions.

## Learn Before Paying

Organizations should be able to experience the platform before making a purchasing decision.

The Free edition exists to demonstrate value rather than severely limiting usability.

---

## Scale Naturally

Organizations should unlock additional capabilities as operational complexity increases.

Growth should feel natural rather than forced.

---

## Charge for Scale

Core operational workflows should not be hidden behind expensive plans.

Customers pay for:

* larger operations
* advanced automation
* enterprise governance
* integrations
* intelligence
* compliance

—not basic maintenance management.

---

## No Artificial Restrictions

MaintainPro avoids arbitrary feature removal.

Instead, higher editions expand capability, automation, intelligence, and operational scale.

# 4. Free Edition

## Target Customer

* Individuals
* Small businesses
* Pilot deployments
* Product evaluation

---

## Included Modules

* Identity & Access
* Organization Management
* Facility Management
* Asset Management
* Maintenance Operations
* Notifications
* Basic Reporting

---

## Marketplace

Vendor Marketplace available.

Vendor subscriptions are optional.

---

## Operational Limits

Examples (subject to future adjustment):

* 1 Organization
* 1 Facility
* 100 Assets
* 5 Internal Users
* Basic Notifications

---

## Intended Goal

Allow organizations to experience the complete MaintainPro workflow with limited operational scale.

---

# 5. Starter Edition

## Target Customer

Organizations managing one or several facilities.

---

## Adds

* Preventive Maintenance
* Vendor Marketplace enhancements
* Contract Management
* Subscription Billing
* Increased operational limits

---

## Intended Goal

Support organizations running structured maintenance operations.

---

# 6. Professional Edition

## Target Customer

Growing organizations operating across multiple facilities.

---

## Adds

* Advanced Scheduling
* SLA Monitoring
* Vendor Performance
* Advanced Dashboards
* Operational Analytics
* API Access
* Workflow Automation
* Higher operational limits

---

## Intended Goal

Support operational excellence at organizational scale.

---

# 7. Enterprise Edition

## Target Customer

Large organizations with governance, compliance, and integration requirements.

---

## Adds

* Single Sign-On (SSO)
* Enterprise Integrations
* Advanced Security Controls
* Compliance Management
* Advanced Audit
* Custom Roles
* Multi-region Support (future)
* AI Insights (future)
* IoT Integrations (future)
* Digital Twin Support (future)

---

## Intended Goal

Provide enterprise-grade operational governance without changing the core product.

# 9. Future Premium Capabilities

Some future capabilities may be edition-specific.

Examples include:

* Predictive Maintenance
* AI Operational Assistant
* IoT Device Management
* Digital Twins
* Advanced Compliance Packs
* Blockchain Audit Verification
* Smart Contract Vendor Agreements
* Stablecoin Subscription Payments

These capabilities will remain optional and should enhance—not replace—the core MaintainPro experience.

---

# 10. Upgrade Philosophy

Customers should upgrade because:

* they are growing,
* they require additional operational capabilities,
* they need enterprise governance,

—not because essential maintenance workflows are unavailable.

MaintainPro should reward organizational growth rather than penalize smaller customers.

---

# 11. Guiding Principle

> **Every edition should deliver complete operational value. Higher editions expand intelligence, automation, governance, and scale—not basic functionality.**
