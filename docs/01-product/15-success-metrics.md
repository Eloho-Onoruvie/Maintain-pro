# MaintainPro Success Metrics

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Living Document
**Document Type:** Product Specification

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the measurements used to evaluate the success of MaintainPro.

Success is determined by customer outcomes rather than feature delivery.

Every major capability should contribute to one or more measurable business objectives.

These metrics provide a consistent framework for evaluating product effectiveness, customer value, operational performance, and long-term business growth.

---

# 2. Success Philosophy

MaintainPro measures success using outcomes rather than activity.

The product succeeds when organizations operate more efficiently, make better decisions, and maintain healthier infrastructure.

Shipping features is not success.

Improving customer operations is success.

---

# 3. Product Success Categories

MaintainPro evaluates success across six dimensions:

* Product Adoption
* Operational Efficiency
* Customer Success
* Business Growth
* Platform Quality
* Innovation

---

# 4. Product Adoption

Measures how effectively customers adopt the platform.

## Key Metrics

* Organizations onboarded
* Active organizations
* Monthly active users
* Daily active users
* User retention
* User invitation acceptance rate
* Feature adoption rate
* Time to first completed workflow

---

## Success Objective

Organizations should become operational quickly and continue using MaintainPro as their primary operational platform.

---

# 5. Operational Efficiency

Measures improvements in customer operations.

## Key Metrics

* Service request response time
* Work order completion time
* Preventive maintenance completion rate
* Average maintenance backlog
* Asset downtime
* Maintenance cost reduction
* Technician productivity
* SLA compliance rate

---

## Success Objective

Organizations should complete maintenance faster, reduce downtime, and improve operational planning.

---

# 6. Customer Success

Measures the value customers receive.

## Key Metrics

* Customer satisfaction (CSAT)
* Net Promoter Score (NPS)
* Customer retention
* Subscription renewal rate
* Customer support resolution time
* Customer health score

---

## Success Objective

Customers should view MaintainPro as an essential operational platform.

---

# 7. Business Growth

Measures the commercial success of MaintainPro.

## Key Metrics

* Free to Starter conversion rate
* Starter to Professional upgrades
* Professional to Enterprise upgrades
* Monthly recurring revenue (MRR)
* Annual recurring revenue (ARR)
* Customer lifetime value (LTV)
* Customer acquisition cost (CAC)
* Vendor subscriptions
* Marketplace transaction volume

---

## Success Objective

The platform should grow sustainably while delivering increasing customer value.

---

# 8. Marketplace Success

* Vendor award rate
* Average vendor rating
* Marketplace utilization

Organizations should consistently find qualified vendors while vendors receive meaningful business opportunities.

# 9. Platform Quality

# 10. Operational Intelligence

Measures the effectiveness of analytics and decision support.

## Key Metrics

* Dashboard usage
* Report generation
* Preventive maintenance compliance
* Asset health improvements

---

---

# 11. Innovation Metrics

Future platform evolution may be measured using:

* AI-assisted workflow adoption
* Predictive maintenance accuracy
* IoT device adoption
* Smart automation usage
* Integration adoption
* Blockchain verification usage (if implemented)

These metrics become relevant only after the associated capabilities are released.

---

# 12. North Star Metric

The primary success metric for MaintainPro is:

## Operational Work Completed Through MaintainPro

This measures the percentage of an organization's maintenance and operational activities that are managed end-to-end within the platform.

As this percentage increases:

* operational visibility improves
* historical records become more complete
* reporting becomes more accurate
* customer dependence on MaintainPro increases

This represents the platform's overall value better than any single financial or usage metric.

---

# 13. Product Review Cadence

Success metrics should be reviewed regularly.

### Weekly

* Platform quality
* Operational health
* Critical incidents

---

### Monthly

* Product adoption
* Customer success
* Marketplace health
* Business performance

---

### Quarterly

* Product roadmap progress
* Strategic objectives
* Long-term customer outcomes

---

# 14. Guiding Principle

Every feature introduced into MaintainPro should improve at least one measurable success metric.

Features that cannot demonstrate measurable customer or business value should be reconsidered before implementation.

---

# Guiding Principle

> **MaintainPro measures success by improving the operational performance of its customers, not by increasing the number of features it contains.**
