**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Draft
**Document Type:** Product Specification

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the organizations and users that MaintainPro is designed to serve.

Understanding the target customer ensures that every product decision, workflow, and feature addresses real operational needs rather than hypothetical use cases.

MaintainPro is built for organizations responsible for managing physical infrastructure, operational assets, maintenance teams, and external service providers.

---

# 2. Primary Market

MaintainPro primarily serves medium to large organizations that own, operate, or maintain physical facilities and assets.

These organizations typically:

* operate multiple facilities
* manage numerous physical assets
* coordinate maintenance teams
* engage external contractors or vendors
* require operational accountability
* maintain compliance obligations
* depend on reliable infrastructure for daily operations

# 8. Customer Characteristics

MaintainPro is designed for organizations that value:

* operational efficiency
* preventive maintenance
* accountability
* regulatory compliance
* measurable performance
* centralized operational visibility
* vendor collaboration
* long-term asset management

Organizations seeking only a simple task tracker are outside the primary target market.

# 10. Customer Growth

MaintainPro should grow alongside its customers.

Organizations should be able to expand from:

* one facility to hundreds
* dozens of assets to hundreds of thousands
* a handful of users to thousands
* local vendors to nationwide service networks

without changing platforms.

---

# 11. Customer Success

MaintainPro creates value when customers can:

* reduce downtime
* increase asset availability
* standardize maintenance operations
* improve vendor accountability
* simplify compliance
* lower maintenance costs
* improve operational planning
* make confident decisions using trusted operational data

# Guiding Principle

> **MaintainPro is built for organizations that depend on reliable physical operations and require a scalable, accountable, and intelligent platform to manage them.**
